How Global Intelligence Hub works

This personal research directory indexes publicly available RSS and Atom metadata: original article title, publisher, URL and publication date. It does not reproduce full copyrighted articles or claim to have verified that every source is free to read.

Morning and evening

Scheduled collection jobs are configured for 07:30 and 18:30 India time (UTC cron). Morning/evening classification refers to when a record was first indexed, not when the underlying story occurred. Publication depends on the scheduled workflow completing successfully. GitHub Actions schedules can be delayed or missed.

Search

Archive search is local, with limited keyword synonym expansion. “Search the web” opens Google News search results in a new tab. It does not automatically ingest those results or provide unlimited API access.

Free-access target

At least 90% free-to-read content is an editorial target, not a guarantee. Access remains “Unknown” unless explicitly verified. RSS availability does not prove an article is freely readable.

Exports

CSV includes structured article metadata. Word downloads a Word-compatible .doc document, not a native .docx file. PDF uses your browser’s print-to-PDF capability. Bookmark storage is device-local; export and restore JSON backups to move bookmarks between devices.

Privacy and backups

No account or analytics are included. Bookmarks are stored in your browser only. The article index is a portable JSON file. For multi-device bookmark synchronization, a database and authentication would need to be added.

Feed health

Publisher feeds sometimes move, reject automated requests, or fail temporarily. The dashboard shows the number of feeds successfully fetched on the most recent run. A failing feed does not prevent other sources from updating.
